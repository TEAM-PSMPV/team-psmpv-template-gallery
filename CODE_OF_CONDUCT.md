# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TEAM-PSMPV is committed to a professional, welcoming, and harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, give actionable feedback, protect confidential information, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, credential exposure, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a person who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project spaces, and other public or private interactions conducted on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the TEAM-PSMPV [contact form](https://www.teampsmpv.com/contact-us). Do not publicly identify the affected person or post sensitive evidence. Maintainers will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove access depending on severity and pattern.

Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
